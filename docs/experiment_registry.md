## ESR reproducibility check

When an older CoreAudioML ESR is available, the pipeline compares it against the independently recomputed ESR with tolerance 1e-5.

Validated against previous ESR values: **14/14** within tolerance.

## Notes

- Lower is better for ESR, MAE, MSE, RMSE, MR-STFT and LUFS error.
- best_val remains useful for checkpoint selection and convergence, but is not the common cross-architecture fidelity metric.
- Computational benchmarks (parameters, inference time, RTF and peak inference memory) will be added in the dedicated efficiency benchmark stage.
